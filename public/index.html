How To Play

WASD move your colony core around the map Mouse aim your worker swarm and extend your control range Space hatch a new worker when you have eggs Q merge 2 workers into 1 stronger worker F split 1 large worker into 2 smaller faster workers Left Click hold to send workers into raid mode and direct them Shift burst forward by spending score instead of health

Large green circles can be eaten by your hive or by workers that grow large enough.
