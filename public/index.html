    Latest Update

    Colony.io v1.1.0

    A smoother, larger, and more readable arena update focused on worker control, custom games, network feel, and HUD clarity.

    Gameplay

    • Worker merging now combines worker size so merged workers become visibly stronger.
    • Workers now show HP bars below them for clearer combat reads.
    • Improved worker speed, growth, splitting, hatch bounce, and food collision behavior.
    • Hive eliminations now steal part of the defeated hive's score.
    • Added kill streak support, kill sounds, larger maps, and a 20,000 score round target.

    Cards & Buffs

    • Added run-based card buff choices with Uncommon, Rare, Epic, and Legendary rarities.
    • Legendary choices appear at major run milestones.
    • Active buffs now appear in a compact HUD strip with hover descriptions.

    Network & Custom Games

    • Improved live snapshot handling for smoother gameplay.
    • Fixed custom games where players could appear unable to move.
    • Added custom room settings for player count, resources, score goal, map size, and damage tuning.
    • Network HUD now keeps the focus on quick Ping readability.

    UI, Audio & Spectate

    • Refined HUD layout, leaderboard, How To Play panel, buff display, and 100% browser zoom behavior.
    • Added music, sound effects, volume controls, mute, and low graphics options.
    • Added spectate mode for watching matches while dead or without joining the arena.
